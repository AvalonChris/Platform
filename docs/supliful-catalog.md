# Supliful catalogue notes

Product details collected from the Supliful account while choosing the launch lineup. Prices are what
Supliful charges us, not retail prices. Gathered 2026-10-02/03; check the account for current figures.

The figures the store actually calculates with live in the admin: each product's supplier cost and
shipping weight on its product page, the shared fees and shipping table under **Costs**, and the
results under **Profit**. This file is the reference for where those numbers came from.

## Fees

**Plans.** Free can't fulfil customer orders (samples only). Pro ($69/month, $49/month annual) is
required, and our custom store needs a Shopify Basic store as the order relay.

**Fulfilment fee, per order.** $1.99 for the first unit of each distinct product listing, $1.29 for each
additional unit of the same product. Separate listings built on the same base product each pay the $1.99,
so create each product only once in Supliful (stacks are sent as their components, not as listings).

**Processing fee.** 2.99% of each payment we make to Supliful (product + fulfilment + shipping), charged
at the point of purchase. Waived only under a signed invoice-billing agreement, which isn't available by
default.

**Total cost of an order to us** = products + fulfilment fees + shipping, plus 2.99% of that sum.

  30 servings per container (30-day supply). Image: `Files/SupplementFacts/nmn-capsules.png`.
- **Ingredients:** β-Nicotinamide Mononucleotide (500 mg), HPMC (vegetable capsule), Microcrystalline
  Cellulose, Silicon Dioxide, Magnesium Stearate.
- **Weight:** net 36 g; gross 45 g (0.1 lb).
- **Made in:** USA. Ships to the US only.
- **Suggested use:** As a dietary supplement, adults take one (1) capsule daily. For best results, take
  with 6-8 oz (177-237ml) of water or as directed by healthcare professional.
- **Warnings:** Do not exceed recommended dose. Consult a physician if pregnant, nursing, under 18, or
  have a medical condition. Keep out of reach of children. Do not use if safety seal is damaged or
  missing. Store in a cool, dry place.
